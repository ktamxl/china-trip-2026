Shenzhen Talent Park

人才公園 · 南山区后海片区

Shenzhen's most scenic lake-side park, designed around a ring of water and a crescent moon bridge. Walk the lakeside boardwalk at sunset — the CBD skyline reflected on the water creates one of Shenzhen's most photographed scenes. Free entry, open 6 AM – 11 PM.

Shenzhen Bay Park

深圳灣公園 · 南山区望海路

A 13-kilometre seaside urban park along Shenzhen Bay. One of the city's most beloved green spaces — palm-lined promenades, ocean breezes, and views across the bay to Hong Kong. The sunset here is spectacular. Best accessed from Houhai section (South). Free entry.

🌊 Seaside 🚴 13 km Promenade 🌅 Free

3

Safari Park Shenzhen

野生動物園 · 南山区西丽湖东侧 4065号

China's first zoo with uncaged animals — visitors drive through savanna-style enclosures. Home to over 300 species including giant pandas, golden snub-nosed monkeys, and rare white tigers. 640,000 sqm of natural landscape. One of China's top-rated family attractions. Allow a full day.

OCT Harbour

歡樂港灣 · 南山区望海路2008号

Shenzhen's most beloved waterfront entertainment district. A lakeside paradise with boutique shops, international restaurants, a huge indoor theme park, and a musical fountain. The waterfront at night, lit up, is genuinely magical — ranked #5 family attraction in Guangdong. Free to walk around.

Gankeng Hakka Town

甘坑客家小镇 · 龙岗区甘坑社区甘李路18号

A beautifully preserved Hakka village turned cultural tourism destination. The town has retained its traditional Hakka architecture —古老的围龙屋, stone-paved lanes, and canals. Try authentic Hakka cuisine (盐焗鸡, 客家酿豆腐) and browse handcraft workshops. Ranked #5 spring outing destination in Guangdong.

Shenzhen Library North Hall

深圳圖書館北館 · 龍華區騰龍路30號

Shenzhen's newest major cultural institution — a stunning white building in Longhua District designed to hold 8 million books with 2,500 reading seats. The counterpart to the main library in Futian. World's First Robot 6S Store

未来时代机器人6S店 · 龙岗区坂田街道雅星路星河WORLD

The world's first Robot 6S Store — a 2,000 sqm immersive showroom that redefines what retail can feel like. Think of it as the automotive 4S concept reimagined for embodied AI: Sales, Spare parts, Service, Survey (information feedback), plus Rental and Customisation. Robots here shake your hand, make you fresh pancakes and coffee, dance, play piano, write calligraphy, and run real-world training scenarios with 100+ humanoids training simultaneously. Robots greet you at the door; the whole experience feels like stepping two years into the future. A global media sensation since its July 2025 opening. Allow 1.5–2 hours.

Futian CBD Civic Centre Light Show

深圳市民中心灯光秀 · 福田区中心区

Shenzhen's most iconic free light show — the city's landmark skyscrapers become a synchronized canvas of coloured light, music, and moving imagery. The show runs on the façades of the CBD towers and is visible from both street level (市民广场/市民中心广场) and from above at Lianhua Hill (蓮花山) for a panoramic view over the glowing skyline. Rated among the top free urban light shows in China.

🌃 Night Light Shows — Schedule, Booking & What's Actually Running

Shenzhen's signature free evening spectacle: entire CBD skylines turned into synchronised light canvases. Two shows matter for this trip — and the important detail is that right now only one of them is running.

⚠️ Futian Civic Centre show is dark. Suspended since 30 Apr 2026 for deep maintenance and expected back on 1 Oct 2026 (National Day). By our dates (28 Nov – 1 Dec) it should be running again — but re-check before we go.

1 · Futian Civic Centre 福田市民中心灯光秀 — the flagship

  • What it is — the city's largest building-façade light show, animated in sync across the CBD skyline around Civic Center Plaza.
  • Nights — Fridays, Saturdays and statutory holidays · 3 shows a night · ~15 minutes each.
  • Times — Apr–Oct 19:30 / 20:30 / 21:30 · Nov–Mar 19:00 / 20:00 / 21:00 — our window is the winter schedule.
  • Not shown on — Qingming holiday, 7 Jul, 18 Sep, 30 Sep, 13 Dec (interior lighting only). Cancelled in typhoon, rain or lightning warnings.
  • Tickets — free, but Civic Center Plaza needs advance booking via the WeChat mini-program 「福田中心区夜景灯光预约导引服务」. Booking opens 3 days ahead at 10:00 for each session.
  • No-booking viewing spots — Lianhua Mountain summit (best overhead panorama, crowded), Shenzhen Children's Palace entrance, and Yitian Rd / Jintian Rd northbound for the "inside it" view.
  • Getting there — Metro Lines 2 / 4, Civic Center Station, Exits A / F → 3–5 min walk to the plaza.

2 · Talent Park 人才公园灯光秀 — the one over the water

  • What it is — the show at Talent Park in Nanshan, watched across the lake with the Shenzhen Bay / Houhai skyline behind it: the "Spring Bamboo" (China Resources) tower plus Alibaba, Baidu and Tencent HQ lights reflecting on the water. Gentler and about 180° wide — far less overwhelming than Futian.
  • Nights — Fridays, Saturdays and statutory holidays · ~15 minutes per session.
  • Times — Apr–Oct 19:30 / 20:30 / 21:30 · Nov–Mar 19:00 / 20:00 / 21:00.
  • Tickets — free, no reservation needed — just walk in.
  • Best viewing spots — 星光桥 Starlight Bridge, π桥 Pi Bridge, and 无忧广场 Wuyou Square inside the park.
  • Address — Talent Park, Yuehai Subdistrict, Nanshan District.
  • Getting there — Metro Line 13 Talent Park Station Exit B / B1 → ~200 m to the north gate (closest); Lines 2 / 11 Houhai Station Exit H → ~1.2 km; Line 2 Dengliang Station Exit C → ~1 km (west gate).
  • Notes — cancelled in rain. The park adjoins Shenzhen Bay Park, so it pairs perfectly with an evening seaside walk. This is also Stop 1 on this page.
✅ Talent Park is the reliable choice while the Civic Centre show is under repair — free, no booking, and the better view for photographs.
